Supporting Our Community, Preserving Our Environment

Tiyeni Group is deeply dedicated to making a positive and lasting impact on the Victoria Falls community by actively working to uplift and empower its residents. The company believes in the importance of fostering sustainable development and social well-being, and this runs Afro Edge Zimbabwe, a not-for-profit organization committed to addressing critical issues such as education, healthcare, environmental sustainability, and social services.
